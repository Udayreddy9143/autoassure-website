# The Automation Readiness Checklist
### 10 questions that show you where manual work is putting your releases at risk — from AutoAssure

Score 1 point for every "yes." Add them up at the end.

1. Can you name every manual, repetitive step your team still does by hand before a release ships?
2. If your app broke in production right now, could you say within 10 minutes *which layer* broke — UI, API, data, or infra?
3. Does anyone other than the developer who wrote a feature independently validate it before it ships?
4. Do you have an automated test suite that covers your critical end-to-end user flows, not just isolated units?
5. Can your regression suite run unattended and finish in under an hour?
6. When data moves between environments or systems, is it validated automatically — or does someone eyeball a spreadsheet?
7. Do failed tests block a release automatically, or does someone have to remember to check?
8. If a customer reports a data or calculation error, can you prove — with logs or test evidence — whether it was already covered by a test?
9. Has your team's QA headcount grown slower than your release frequency (i.e., automation is doing more of the work over time)?
10. Could you hand your test suite and validation scripts to a new hire and have them understand what's covered within a day?

---

## Scoring

**8-10: Strong foundation.** You likely need targeted automation, not a rebuild — happy to point out the highest-leverage gaps.

**4-7: Growing risk.** Manual work is still absorbing hours every release cycle, and something will eventually slip through it.

**0-3: High exposure.** Bugs are probably reaching production because there's no systematic net catching them before your customers do.
